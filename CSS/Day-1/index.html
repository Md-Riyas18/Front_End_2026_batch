Menu 📜

Espresso ☕

Espresso is one of the most popular and classic types of coffee, known for its strong flavour, rich aroma, and smooth texture. It is carefully prepared by passing hot water through finely ground, high-quality coffee beans under pressure. The result is a small but powerful cup of coffee with a rich taste and a beautiful layer of crema on top. Espresso can be enjoyed on its own or used as the base for other favourite drinks such as Cappuccino, Latte, and Americano. At Coffee House, we prepare every cup of Espresso with freshly ground beans to give our customers a warm, refreshing, and satisfying coffee experience.

Cappuccino ☕

Cappuccino is a popular coffee drink that is loved for its rich flavour, creamy texture, and smooth taste. It is traditionally made with a shot of strong espresso, steamed milk, and a thick layer of milk foam on top. The combination of strong coffee and soft, creamy milk creates a perfect balance of flavour. Cappuccino is often enjoyed in the morning or during a relaxing break with friends and family. At Coffee House, we prepare our Cappuccino using freshly ground coffee beans and carefully steamed milk to create a delicious and satisfying cup. Whether you prefer a strong coffee taste or a creamy and smooth drink, our Cappuccino is a perfect choice for every coffee lover.

Latte ☕

Latte is a smooth and creamy coffee drink that is enjoyed by people all around the world. It is traditionally prepared with a shot of rich espresso and a larger amount of steamed milk, finished with a light layer of milk foam on top. The combination of strong espresso and warm, creamy milk creates a mild and balanced flavour that is perfect for people who prefer a softer coffee taste. Latte can also be enjoyed with different flavours such as vanilla, caramel, or chocolate, making it easy to choose according to your preference. At Coffee House, we prepare our Latte with freshly ground coffee beans and carefully steamed milk to create a warm, smooth, and delicious coffee experience for every customer.

Americano ☕

Americano is a classic coffee drink known for its simple preparation and rich, refreshing taste. It is made by combining a shot of strong espresso with hot water, creating a smooth coffee that is lighter than straight espresso but still has a delicious coffee flavour. The amount of water can be adjusted according to personal preference, making the drink stronger or milder. Americano is a great choice for people who enjoy the natural taste and aroma of coffee without adding too much milk. At Coffee House, we prepare our Americano using freshly brewed espresso and hot water to provide a warm, smooth, and satisfying coffee experience.

Mocha 🍫

>

Mocha is a delicious coffee drink that combines the rich taste of espresso with the sweet and creamy flavour of chocolate. It is usually prepared with freshly brewed espresso, steamed milk, and chocolate syrup or cocoa, creating a perfect balance between strong coffee and sweet chocolate. A layer of milk foam or whipped cream can be added on top to make the drink even more enjoyable. Mocha is a popular choice for people who love both coffee and chocolate. At Coffee House, we prepare our Mocha with quality coffee beans, smooth chocolate, and creamy milk to create a warm, rich, and satisfying drink that is perfect for relaxing or enjoying with friends.

Cold Brew ☕

Cold Brew is a refreshing coffee drink that is prepared by slowly steeping freshly ground coffee beans in cold or room-temperature water for several hours. Unlike regular hot coffee, Cold Brew is made without using hot water, which gives it a smooth, naturally sweet, and less bitter taste. It is usually served chilled with ice and can be enjoyed plain or with milk, cream, or a little sweetness according to personal preference. Cold Brew is a perfect choice for hot days when you want something cool and refreshing while still enjoying the rich flavour of coffee. At Coffee House, we carefully prepare our Cold Brew using quality coffee beans to create a smooth, refreshing, and enjoyable drink.
