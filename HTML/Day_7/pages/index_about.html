About :

Softlogic Systems (SLA) is a prominent software development and IT training institute headquartered in Chennai, India. Established initially as a software company in 2001, it expanded in 2005 into an educational hub dedicated to bridging the gap between collegiate academic learning and practical IT industry standards.

💡 Core Services & Offerings

  • IT Career Training: They offer customized online and classroom training across more than 70 distinct tech fields.
  • Global Certifications: As an authorized learning provider, they partner with major global tech brands to provide specialized certification paths including IBM Global Certifications and Microsoft Official Tracks.
  • Job Placement Assurance: SLA provides 100% placement training and lifelong, unlimited interview access with their network of over 500 IT multinational and hiring corporate partners.
  • Industry Mentorship: Students are trained by a collective of more than 100 industry experts who carry years of live development experience.

📚 Popular Training Programs

  • oftware Development: Java Full Stack, MERN Stack, Python, .NET, and Android.
  • Data Science & Analytics: Core Data Science, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Power BI.Cloud & DevOps: AWS DevOps, Microsoft Azure, and Cloud Computing architecture.
  • Software Testing: Manual testing, Selenium, API Testing, and Appium Automation.Business Automation & Digital: Salesforce, Automation Anywhere (RPA), and Digital Marketing.
