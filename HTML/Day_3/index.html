THE STORY IS ALL ABOUT MY EXPERIENCE SO LET'S START

1)MY COLLEGE TRIP – ONE OF MY BEST MEMORIES

I Want To Share One Of My Best Memories From My College Days. In My College, We Were The First Batch Of AI & DS. Before Joining College, I Studied In A Co-Educational School From LKG To 12th Standard. But Honestly, During My School Days, I Never Spoke To A Single Girl. There Was Almost No Conversation Between Boys And Girls. Everyone Had Their Own Work, And We Just Focused On Our Own Things. I Was Also A Very Shy Person, Especially When It Came To Talking To Girls. I Didn’t Know How To Start A Conversation Or How To Build A Friendship. So, If A Girl Spoke To Me, I Would Simply Answer Her. Sometimes, I Couldn’t Even Find The Right Words To Respond. Because Of That, Some People Thought I Had An Attitude. But Only I Knew What Was Actually Going On In My Mind. When I Joined College, I Thought Things Would Be Completely Different. I Thought, “Now That I’m In College, Everyone Will Talk To Each Other, And We Will All Become Like One Family.” But Later, I Realized That It Was Almost The Same As School. We Didn’t Really Gel With The Other Classes. However, I Noticed That Students From Some Other Classes Were Very Close To Each Other, Almost Like A Family. So, We Thought That Going On A College Trip Together Would Help Us Get Closer And Improve Our Communication, Especially Between The Boys And Girls. We Planned A Trip Every Year, But For Some Reason, The Plan Was Never Executed. Somehow, Three Years Passed, And During Our 6th Semester, We Finally Made A Plan To Go To Kerala For A Three-Day Trip During The Holidays. We Planned Everything, But Our HOD Didn’t Approve The Trip. However, We Got Permission From Our Class Coordinator, So We Decided To Go Anyway. The Trip Started At 9:00 PM At Night. We Travelled By Bus And Reached Vagamon At Around 7:00 AM The Next Morning. After Reaching There, We Checked Into Our Rooms, Freshened Up, And Had Breakfast. After Breakfast, The Jeeps Were Ready For An Off-Road Trip In Vagamon. We Were Really Excited And Enjoyed The Off-Road Ride A Lot. The Organizers Divided Everyone Into Groups, But Unfortunately, My Friends And I Arrived Late, So They Had Already Taken The Group Photo Without Us. That Night, The Event Organizers Arranged A Campfire And DJ Night. Everyone Was Excited And Ready To Enjoy The Evening. Some People Started Dancing, But I Didn’t Know How To Start A Conversation, And I Definitely Didn’t Know How To Dance. At That Moment, My Mind Was Like, “WTF, Riyas! Did You Just Come Here To Eat And Sleep? Come On! You Won’t Get Opportunities Like This Every Day!” But Still, I Didn’t Dance. After The Dancing Was Over, We Took Some Pictures. Then, The Girls Suggested That We All Sit Around The Campfire And Sing Songs Together. I Was Really Excited Because I Knew How To Sing A Little Bit. So, We All Sat Around The Campfire And Started Singing Songs Together. That Was Actually The Moment When We Started To Gel With Each Other. We Talked, Laughed, Sang Songs, And Enjoyed The Moment Together. Finally, We Went To Sleep At Around 1:00 AM. On The Second Day, We Explored Vagamon For A While And Then Travelled Towards Kochi. There Weren’t Many Activities That Day, So It Was More Of A Travelling And Relaxing Day. On The Third Day, We Reached Kochi. We Visited The Beach, Went On A Boat Ride From The Harbour, And Visited Lulu Mall. We Covered Almost All The Planned Places That Day. Finally, The Trip Came To An End. But Something Unexpected Happened During The Trip. Some Of My Friends Bought UNO Cards. Surprisingly, Those UNO Cards Helped Us Stay Connected Even After The Trip Was Over. After We Returned To College, Whenever We Had Free Time During Class, Six Boys And Six Girls Would Play UNO Together. Some Of The Other Guys Played Normal Cards, Some Played Raja And Rani, And Others Played Box Cricket Inside The Classroom. During Those Moments, Something Really Special Happened To Me. For The First Time In My College Life, After Three Years, I Got A Female Friend. And Now, She Is One Of My Best Friends. I Don’t Even Treat Her Differently Because She Is A Girl. I Treat Her Just Like One Of My Guy Friends. I Even Got Used To Calling Her “Daa,” Just Like I Do With My Other Friends. Looking Back, This College Trip Was Not Just About Visiting Kerala, Travelling, Taking Photos, Or Having Fun. For Me, It Was About Breaking Out Of My Comfort Zone, Becoming More Confident, Building Friendships, And Finally Learning How To Connect With People. That’s Why This Trip Will Always Be One Of My Best Memories From College.

2) TOPIC ABOUT ANIMEEE :

If The Topic Is Anime, I’ve Got Some Fire To Share! Anime Is Not Just About Entertainment. It Has Taught Me A Lot Of Lessons That I Sometimes Couldn’t Learn From Other People. I’m Not Hyping Or Exaggerating Anime When I Say This. Even During Some Of The Lowest Moments Of My Life, Watching Anime Really Helped Me Overcome Difficult Situations. It Taught Me How To Handle Situations When There Seems To Be Nothing I Can Do, How To Stay Calm And Silent Even When The Situation Gets Worse, And How To Keep Moving Forward. Anime Has Also Taught Me How To Behave In Friendships, How To Respect Others, And How To Deal With People Who Demotivate Me. Sometimes, People May Tell You, “You Can’t Do That,” Or “That’s Too Difficult For You.” But Instead Of Listening To Those Words, Focus On What You Want To Achieve. Success Doesn’t Come Easily. Patience Is Important. You May Have To Work Hard For A Long Time, And The Process May Be Slow, But One Day, You Can Achieve Your Goal. Trust Your Progress. There Are So Many Powerful Quotes In Anime That Motivate Me And Sometimes Remind Me To Discipline Myself: “Don’t Give Up On Your Goal. You’re Still Alive. If You Give Up On Your Dream, It’s Only Because You’re Dead.” “Never Go Back On Your Word.” “We Never Know Which Choice Is Right, So Choose The One You Won’t Regret.” “Give Up On Your Dreams And Die.” These Are Just A Few Examples. Anime Has So Many Quotes And Lessons About Friendship, Discipline, Patience, Courage, Failure, Respect, Sacrifice, And Never Giving Up. That’s Why I Would Suggest Giving Anime A Chance. Don’t Watch It Only For Entertainment. Sometimes, Behind The Action, Comedy, And Story, There Are Real-Life Lessons That Can Change The Way You Think And Help You Face Difficult Situations. Anime Is Not Just Something I Watch. Sometimes, It’s Something I Learn From.
